## 1. Who We Are

Prepped 4 Prep ("we", "us", "our") supplies back-to-school stationery preparation, book-covering, and custom labeling services to parents and schools in Durban, South Africa.

**Information Officer:** [NAME], reachable at [EMAIL]. *(POPIA requires every organisation processing personal information to designate an Information Officer, registered with the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za. This must be done before launch — it isn't optional.)*

## 2. What Personal Information We Collect

When you place an order through our website, we collect:

- **About you (the parent/guardian):** name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and a payment reference (we never see or store your card details — see Section 5).
- **About your child:** first and last name, school, and grade level. This is provided by you, the parent or guardian, and is used solely to prepare and label the correct order for your child.

## 3. Why We Collect It

We only collect what's needed to fulfil your order:

- To build the correct stationery list for your child's school and grade.
- To print accurate name labels on your child's items and box.
- To process payment and generate a receipt/invoice.
- To deliver or notify you when your order is ready for collection.
- To send order updates via WhatsApp and/or email (order received, packed, ready, delivered).

We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without separately asking, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

## 4. Your Consent

We ask for your explicit, opt-in consent at checkout before your order is placed. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (Section 9), though this may affect our ability to fulfil an order already in progress.

## 5. How We Protect Your Information

- All data sent between your browser and our website is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Sensitive information stored in our database is encrypted at rest.
- Payment card details are handled entirely by PayFast, our payment processor — we never receive or store your card number. We only keep the transaction reference PayFast gives us after a successful payment.
- Access inside our business is role-restricted: for example, warehouse staff packing your order can see your child's name, school, and item list, but cannot see your home address, phone number, or payment details.
- Every significant action taken on your data (such as exporting or deleting a record) is logged with the staff member responsible.

## 6. Who We Share It With

Only the service providers needed to run your order, and only the minimum information each one needs:

- **PayFast** — to process your payment.
- **WhatsApp (Meta) / email (Resend)** — to send you order status updates, if you've opted in.
- **QuickBooks Online** — our own accounting software, to generate invoices/records for our books.

We do not share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## 7. How Long We Keep It

- Order and customer records are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD — e.g. "the current academic year plus 5 years"] to satisfy normal South African financial record-keeping requirements.
- You can request earlier deletion of your information at any time, subject to what we're legally required to retain (e.g. for tax purposes).

## 8. A Note About Children's Information

We collect your child's name, school, and grade directly from you, the parent or guardian — never from the child directly — and only to prepare and correctly label their order. We don't use a child's information for any purpose beyond fulfilling that specific order.

## 9. Your Rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

- Ask what personal information we hold about you and your child.
- Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
- Ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.
- Withdraw consent for us to use your information going forward.
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za) if you believe we've mishandled your information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [EMAIL — e.g. admin@prepped4prep.co.za].

## 10. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent change.
